## Full description (max 4000 characters)

```
Kryvo is a private vault for your photos, videos, audio, passwords and notes —
fully offline and encrypted on your phone.

🔒 100% OFFLINE & PRIVATE
Kryvo has no internet permission. Nothing you hide ever leaves your phone: no
cloud, no account, no ads, no tracking. Everything is encrypted with AES-256.

📷 HIDE PHOTOS, VIDEOS & AUDIO
• Move photos, videos, web images and audio into the vault
• Removes the originals from your gallery
• Folders, favourites ⭐, search and sorting
• Unhide back to your gallery any time
• Recycle Bin, plus recovery of deleted items for 30 days

🧮 DISGUISE THE APP
Kryvo is a private vault that can look like another app. Change its icon and
name to a working Calculator, Clock, Notes, Game or Flashlight. Only your secret
code opens the vault.

🛡️ APP LOCK
Put a PIN or fingerprint on WhatsApp, Gallery, Messages or any app.

📸 INTRUDER SELFIE
Takes a photo of anyone who enters a wrong PIN, with a break-in log.

🎭 FAKE PIN
Forced to open Kryvo? Enter your fake PIN — it opens a separate, nearly empty
vault and your real files stay hidden.

🔑 PASSWORDS & PRIVATE NOTES
Keep passwords and private notes encrypted with your master password. Built-in
password generator.

💾 FULL ENCRYPTED BACKUP
Back up everything into one encrypted file and save it to Google Drive or your
phone. Restore it on a new phone with your master password.

✨ AND MORE
• PIN, password and fingerprint unlock
• Recovery key if you forget your password
• Shake or flip the phone to lock instantly
• Folder lock with a separate PIN
• Share photos straight into Kryvo from any app
• 15 themes, 18 languages
• Screenshots of the vault are blocked

Kryvo is made by Faraz Labs. Questions or ideas? Use Feedback inside the app.
```

2. A photo folder with a few harmless photos — "Hide photos & videos"
3. App icon & disguise screen — "Looks like a calculator"
4. The Calculator disguise itself — "Only your code opens the vault"
5. App Lock list — "Lock WhatsApp, Gallery & more"
6. Intruder alert with a photo — "See who tried to get in"
7. Full backup screen — "Encrypted backup to Google Drive"
8. Themes screen — "15 beautiful themes"

**Tip:** the app blocks screenshots on purpose (FLAG_SECURE). For store
screenshots, ask for a special build with screenshots allowed — do not ship
that build to users.
