Pick your icon

Kryvo colours, or a disguise that really works.

Private by design

Kryvo has no internet permission — it physically cannot send your data anywhere.

Encryption

AES-256-GCM. Passwords are protected by your master password (PBKDF2-HMAC-SHA256, 310,000 rounds). Media keys live in the Android Keystore.

Zero data collected

No account, no analytics, no ads. The Play Store data-safety label reads "No data collected".

Your keys, your data

Forgot the password and no recovery key? Nobody can open the vault — not even us. Set up a recovery key in Settings.
